Cost & insurance

Nobody is turned away

We take Medicaid, Medicare and most commercial plans. If you have no insurance, you pay on a sliding scale based on household size and income, starting at $25 a visit.

Bring photo ID and proof of income if you have them. If you do not have them, come anyway and we will work it out at the desk.
